# Demo script (Fire TV track, 3–4 min)

The demo uses real footage. Three or four people play on a half court in distinct colours. A phone on a tripod films them, the video is uploaded from that phone during the demo, and the rest plays on a Fire TV with the remote.

- **Pipeline:** everything on screen comes out of the live stack. The API runs on Lambda, and the hfbox runner (GPU) processes the game. The TV is the Fire TV APK, and the phone uses the web companion.
- **Backup:** a second copy of the same session, uploaded and processed beforehand, sits on Home. Any beat can fall back to it.
- **Status:** docs/demo-readiness.md has the state of each beat.

## Film it (the day before, and again on the day)

These rules come from what the tracker and the shot detector need (D-89, D-90):

- **Distinct outfits, one colour per person.** For example red, blue, yellow and white tops, not two teams in matching kits. With up to 6 people on court, Replay tells people apart by the colour of their body. Two people in the same colour get swapped (D-89). The top matters most: on a CC clip where three of four players wore black tops and differed only in their shorts, the follow-cam was on the right person 64 % of the time, against 94 % for four different outfits (D-89 round 5).
- **Rim in front of a plain wall or fence, never the sky.** Shots and makes come from the ball at the rim, seen against what is behind it. Bright sky or trees behind the rim hides the ball (D-90).
- **Camera above head height.** Use a tripod at about 2–2.5 m, or on bleachers or a wall, so heads stay below the rim and do not cross it (D-90).
- **Half court, the whole half in frame.** The baseline corners, the key and the rim should be visible. Shoot landscape, and don't move the phone during a clip: a re-aim mid-clip reads as a shot (D-90).
- **Calibrate on the real lines, and keep onlookers out of frame.** The automatic court fit fails on phone footage of a half court, and when it fails Replay keeps everyone in the picture rather than guess (D-94). So drag the calibration dots onto the painted lines by hand; then people off the court are left out. Without that, people behind the fence or on the next court show up as extra players.
- **Keep the clip short.** 1–3 minutes of play: a few makes, a steal, a rebound or two.
- **Players:** film only people who have agreed to it. Unnamed players stay "Player N" (no face matching), and you name them on the phone.

## Before recording

- **Morning list done** (docs/demo-readiness.md, "Morning"):
  - the Lambda is deployed;
  - the runner has been restarted with `REPLAY_PIPELINE=worker`, so the default `REPLAY_WORKER_STAGES` give every upload Highlights, Condensed, Angles, Free view and POV;
  - the new APK is installed;
  - the stale test games are deleted.
- **Backup game:** the same session (or yesterday's clip) uploaded, processed and checked end to end: Replay angles (with Free view and a Player's view cut) and Highlights all appear.
- **Fire TV:** the Replay app is open on Home, and the remote is paired.
  - Record the screen with an HDMI capture card, or with `adb shell screenrecord` (3 min max per file).
- **Phone:** charged, the camera app able to scan QR codes, the clip saved in the gallery.
- **Processing time:** a 45 s clip took about 4 min of wall time on the box with every stage. That was 2026-10-22 on a local worker run like the runner's, and some stages were cached from an earlier attempt. The uncached stage time adds up to about 4.2 min. Expect it to grow with clip length: budget 10–15 min for 2–3 min of play, more if the shared GPU box is busy. Plan the edit around it: start the upload, cut to the backup game, and come back when the game is ready.

Keys on the remote: arrows = D-pad, Select, Back, Play/Pause, Menu (≡) = **Do more**, in every mode. From the video, Up (or Menu) opens **Do more** from the top, and Down opens **Moments** from the bottom. Back always goes up exactly one level. Side panels (Stats, the Player card) close with Left. The chip top-left always says what you are watching ("Following Player 3", "Highlights · 2 of 5"), and the keys that work right now sit bottom-right. On Home, the first Back only shows "Press Back again to exit", so one Back too many no longer drops the demo onto the Fire TV launcher.

## Honest caveats to say or caption

- **Accuracy is real-footage accuracy.**
  - Tracking can swap two players in the same colour, which is why the players wear distinct colours.
  - Makes come from the ball at the rim and miss some shots. The review questions are how the user fixes them.
  - Numbers on the CC BY test footage are in docs/progress.md (D-89, D-90).
- **Virtual replay angles are approximations** and are labelled "Virtual replay" on every frame: the real players' pixels placed on a model of the court. Only the "Camera" cuts are the camera's own pixels.
- **Commentary** is grounded template text from the detected events, not an LLM.
- **Voice ("Replay, clip that" through Alexa)** is not part of this demo.
- **Processing** runs on one GPU box (the hfbox runner), not on AWS GPUs: the account's GPU quota is 0.
