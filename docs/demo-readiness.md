### Fresh upload (beat 14)

- **Add game:** TV Add game → Basketball → QR with `…/u/<id>`. On the phone, the upload went through. In local mode the multipart start answers 409 and the client falls back to a single PUT. That is harmless, but it shows as a console error.
- **Autocalibration** came back in about 6 s: "Found 7 points · 0.5 px · needs a check (score 0.75 < 0.90)". The proposal dots are now numbered 1…n (they all read "0" before tonight's fix).
- **Calibration:** the walk placed points by hand: Full variant, corners nl/nr, rim, then "More points" for key, free-throw and centre (far corners not visible). The worst residual was 12.8 px, because the points came from approximate landmark coordinates rather than a careful drag. While points are added, the API answers 400 for sets too small or too inconsistent to fit. That is by design (the UI shows `ok: false`), but it appears in the console.
- **Processing:** Start processing → all 13 stages done in about 40 s for 45 s of footage (in-process pipeline, box GPU). The TV Add game screen moved on to the game by itself when it was ready, and the phone said "Ready. Watch it on the TV."
- **Home:** the new game "Basketball · Oct 22" leads Home and plays.
- **Opt-in stages:** the hype/highlights, condensed and angles stages are opt-in. They do not run on a plain upload unless the runner has them enabled (see Morning).
